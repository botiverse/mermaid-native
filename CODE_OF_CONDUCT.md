# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Mermaid Native welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background or
identity.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect differing viewpoints, and keep
technical discussions focused on the documented compatibility and security
contracts. Do not publish credentials, private diagram source, or exploit
details in public issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted
sexual attention, and publishing private information without consent are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where possible. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors.
